About Triscaler

Find the credential. Understand the context. Decide what to do.

Triscaler is a macOS security product in development, with a Windows agent coming soon. It helps teams find exposed credentials, see where copies remain and review them with account and source context, including selected AI workflows.

Available today

macOS agent, central analyst console and supported browser checks.

Coming soon

Windows agent. It is not available yet.

Product status

Not a general-availability or independently certified service.

What guides the work

Useful evidence, careful handling of sensitive data and clear statements about product limits.

Why we are building it

Credentials spread through ordinary work.

Passwords and keys get copied into files, exports, source history, screenshots and AI chats. Fixing one copy does not always fix the others. We are building Triscaler to help teams find the copies it supports, understand which account may be affected and see where to start.

I am building Triscaler to make an exposure easier to understand, not just add another alert. It should show what was found, where it appeared and what still needs a person's attention.

Founder, Triscaler

How we work

AI helps, but it does not make the decision.

A local AI embedding model adds context to other checks on the Mac. It cannot create a finding on its own, and scanning does not call a cloud AI service. Supported AI chat records are checked locally against unchanged source files. For some recognized AI apps, Triscaler can report limited connection metadata; it cannot see prompts or encrypted traffic.

When findings are reported, they are sent according to policy and may include the matched value. The server encrypts raw values at rest, keeps them out of ordinary finding lists and records each reveal. We want this boundary to be visible, not hidden behind a privacy slogan.

Current scope

macOS today. Windows agent coming soon.

The current endpoint agent runs on macOS; a Windows agent is planned but not available yet. Browser checks cover selected supported actions. AI-app connection monitoring is best-effort metadata, not content inspection or blocking. Analysts still review findings and decide what action to take. The product does not verify that a credential has been rotated.

Our principles

  • Show the evidenceMake findings traceable to a source and account where known.
  • Keep AI local where possibleScanning and supported chat checks run on the endpoint.
  • Keep people in controlAnalysts review findings and own the next decision.
  • Say what is not builtPlanned features are labeled separately from current product.

Where we hope to go next

LDAP identity validation: a planned server integration to help check account identity and ownership against an organization's directory.

LLM-assisted user-risk profiles: a planned way to summarize relevant security context and provide a risk score for analyst review.

Neither feature is available today. The data used, access controls, retention and human-review safeguards must be defined before either is built.
