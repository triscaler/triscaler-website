Illustrative view

Sensitive data lives in three places. We cover all of them.

Most tools look at one and leave you to guess about the rest. Triscaler was built around all three from the start, and it is where the name comes from.

At rest

Where it is stored

Laptops and workstations, local databases, configuration files, notes, exports, archives, and the screenshots people take of them. The places data quietly accumulates and nobody reviews.

In motion

Where it is going

Clipboard, browser, uploads, removable media, messaging and mail. The moment sensitive data leaves a controlled place for one you do not control.

In use

Who is touching it

Which applications and processes are reading sensitive files, when, and on whose machine, so exposure is caught as it happens rather than at the next audit.

What it finds

Four categories, one queue, ranked so the top of the list is the thing worth doing first.

Credentials and secrets

Passwords, API keys, access tokens, private keys and connection strings left in files, notes, scripts and screenshots. The quiet majority of real breaches start here.

Personal data (PII)

Names, contact details, government identifiers and employee records, wherever they have been copied out of the systems meant to hold them.

Payment and financial data

Card numbers, bank details and financial records, validated so a reference number is not mistaken for a real one.

Regulated and confidential records

Health information, contracts and intellectual property, classified by the policies you set rather than a fixed list we decided for you.

Who is responsible

Every finding names the account it belongs to and the machine it sits on. When the same secret appears across several machines, Triscaler shows where it appeared first, so you are talking to the right person.

Risk you can act on

Findings are scored and ranked, so the queue starts with what matters rather than everything that matched a pattern.

Evidence for auditors

A durable record of what was found, who looked at it, what they decided and when. The trail regulators and customers ask for.

Supports obligations under

GDPR · India DPDP Act · PCI DSS · HIPAA · ISO 27001 · SOC 2

How it works

  1. Deploy: A lightweight agent installs in minutes, with no administrator rights and no change to how people work.
  2. Discover: Continuous discovery across all three data states, not a quarterly scan that is stale by the time it finishes.
  3. Classify: Findings are categorised and scored, with several independent signals required to agree before anything is raised.
  4. Attribute: Each finding is tied to an owner, a machine and a first sighting, so it can be acted on rather than investigated.
  5. Respond: Alert, raise a ticket, or quarantine, with the whole decision trail preserved.

Built so the data never leaves

Analysis happens on the endpoint. Your files, documents and images are never uploaded. Only the redacted finding itself travels, and it is encrypted at rest and in transit.

Quiet enough to be read

A tool that cries wolf gets ignored. Nothing reaches the queue on a single weak signal, and your team can mark anything known-safe so it stops asking.
