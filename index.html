How it works

  1. Ingest: Collect CloudTrail & CloudWatch events into a secure S3 bucket for processing.
  2. Detect: Rule-based and anomaly scoring to prioritize incidents.
  3. Decide: Playbook engine maps detection to staged containment steps and approvals.
  4. Contain: Execute safe actions — suspend keys, quarantine instances, throttle functions.
  5. Audit: Preserve logs & produce a tamper-evident report for forensics.

Designed for minimal risk

Containment actions are staged: monitor → soft-quarantine → hard-quarantine. Human approvals, rollbacks, and clear owner notifications reduce the risk of accidental downtime.
