A note from the founder

AI is changing where credentials show up.

A password can be copied into an AI chat just as easily as into a file. I am building Triscaler to help teams find those copies and decide what to do next.

Founder, Triscaler  ·  What the product does and where it stops

Credentials do not stay in one place.

A key or password can be copied into a file, an old export, a screenshot, source history or an AI chat. Fixing the original does not remove the other copies. Triscaler helps teams find copies in the places it supports.

Not every match is an emergency. The goal is to give people enough context to decide what needs attention.

One finding can have several copies.

A useful alert should show where the value was found and which account may be affected. It should also distinguish one credential seen in several places from several different credentials.

Triscaler groups repeat sightings by credential and account, while keeping each location visible. That helps a team see what to change and where to clean up.

The aim is simple: less time sorting alerts, more time fixing the right thing.

A public view of the system

Architecture, at the level that matters to you.

What can be sent

Some findings can be sent to the console, based on reporting policy. A report may include the matched value. The server encrypts raw values at rest; standard finding lists hide them, and each reveal is recorded.

How Triscaler uses AI.

A small AI model runs on the Mac and adds context to other credential checks. It cannot create a finding by itself. Scanning does not send content to a cloud AI service.

Triscaler checks selected AI chat records stored on the Mac. A chat match only counts when the same credential is verified in an unchanged source file. Chat text is not uploaded, and this is not a warning before a desktop AI prompt is sent.

For some AI apps, Triscaler can note that a connection happened and which app it came from. It cannot see the data sent over that connection, and it does not stop it. This feature is best-effort and can miss activity. Supported browser checks also run locally; text checked for a browser warning is not sent to the console.

What it cannot do yet.

Triscaler is being built for Mac. It does not cover every file, browser action or app. It does not inspect network content or block a connection. It cannot verify that a credential has been changed. A person still needs to review findings and decide what to do.

I want these limits to be clear before anyone relies on the product.

What I want Triscaler to do well.

Show what was found, where else it appears and what changed after someone acted. Keep the evidence clear, protect sensitive details in the interface and leave the decision with the people responsible for it.

We are still building. I will keep sharing what works, what needs work and what we learn from people using the product.

A finding is useful when it helps someone understand the problem and take the next step.
